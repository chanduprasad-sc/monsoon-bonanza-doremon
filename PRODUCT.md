# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Campaign participants using a reward game on their phones. They need a fast, self-explanatory experience that connects eligible investment baskets to Runs scored and campaign rewards.

## Product Purpose

Turn the Doremon Jump incentive campaign into a memorable jump-and-climb game. Players review the basket and reward rules, enter their name, mobile number and branch, steer a mascot by tilting their phone, land on platforms, collect eligible baskets, and watch both score and Runs rise toward campaign slabs.

Difficulty builds gradually: generous fixed boards through 10,000 score points, moving boards from 10,000, breakable boards after 20,000, and tappable flying villains with fireball combat after 30,000. Board density reaches full difficulty at 50,000. A random world—including the Miami beach scene—unlocks every 10,000 score points.

## Brand Personality

Rain-charged, rewarding, and spirited. The experience should feel energetic and competitive while retaining the credibility of a financial-services campaign.

## Anti-references

It must not look like a generic finance dashboard, a casino game, or a direct copy of Doodle Jump artwork. Avoid childish visual clutter, tiny unreadable data, and interactions that work only with one input method.

## Design Principles

- Make the campaign mechanic understandable within five seconds.
- Keep play fair and responsive across tilt, touch, and keyboard controls.
- Celebrate every basket collection with clear business meaning: basket name, fee, Runs, and progress.
- Keep the lead handoff configurable so the campaign team can connect its chosen form or CRM later.
- Preserve trust: accessible contrast, explicit consent copy, and no hidden submission behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for the surrounding interface. Support reduced motion, keyboard control, touch control, and a non-gyroscope fallback. Never rely on color alone for game state.
